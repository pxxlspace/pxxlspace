## Usage

**Settings > Usage** shows compute, bandwidth, and build minutes for the current period, with bandwidth split by project.

- **Compute** beyond your plan's allowance is billed per GB-hour of memory and per vCPU-hour.
- **Free plan bandwidth** has a monthly cap. Serving pauses at the cap and resumes when you upgrade or the month resets.
- **Extra servers** above your plan's included number cost $2 a month each.
- **Extra team seats** can be added beyond your plan's limit.

You only pay for the CPU and memory your project actually deploys with. Lower them any time in **Compute & Scaling**.

## Pay

Pay by card, or from your [wallet](/account/wallet). **Settings > Invoices** lists every invoice, paid and unpaid.
