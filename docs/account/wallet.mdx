title: "Wallet"
description: "Top up a balance and pay for plans, domains, invoices, and usage from it."
---

The wallet holds a balance you can spend on Pxxl. Your balance shows in the dashboard header. Choose **Hide balance** when you share your screen.

Manage it in **Settings > Billing > Wallet**.

## What it pays for

- Plan subscriptions. A plan paid from the wallet renews from the balance each period.
- Domain purchases and renewals.
- Domains you resell through the [domain reseller](/tools/domain-reseller).
- Usage and other invoices.
- Pxxl Points.

## Top up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet">
    Go to **Settings > Billing > Wallet** and choose **Top up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Between $5 and $2,500. A 4% fee is added, never more than $25.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    In Nigeria you pay in naira; elsewhere in US dollars. You can switch currency at checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The balance is kept in US dollars. A naira top-up is converted at Pxxl's current rate. The form shows the fee and the total, in both currencies, before you pay.

### Fee examples

| Top-up | Fee (4%, max $25) | You pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $5 | $0.20 | $5.20 |
| $50 | $2.00 | $52.00 |
| $250 | $10.00 | $260.00 |
| $1,000 | $25.00 | $1,025.00 |
| $2,500 | $25.00 | $2,525.00 |

### After you pay

When the payment clears, the amount is added to your balance and you get a **Wallet topped up** notification with your new balance. The transaction shows in **History**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for payment. |
| **Completed** | Paid and added to your balance. |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it before paying. |
| **Expired** | Not paid within 24 hours. If the payment still arrives later, it's credited anyway. |
| **Failed** | The checkout couldn't be created. Try again. |

You can have up to 5 unpaid top-ups started in an hour. Finish or cancel one to start another.

## Pay bills automatically

Turn on **Pay bills from your balance** (off by default). Pxxl then pays unpaid compute, storage, bandwidth over your plan, and extra servers from your balance, oldest first. It runs as bills arrive and after every top-up.

Purchases you start yourself, like build-minute packs, are never paid automatically.

## Good to know

- The balance can't go below zero. If it's too low, the payment fails and you can pay another way.
- If a plan renewal from the wallet fails, it's treated like a declined card. Top up and retry.
- There are no low-balance emails yet. Check your balance before a renewal.
