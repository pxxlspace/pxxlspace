Every project gets a free Pxxl subdomain. Add your own domain when you are ready to go live.

Open **Domains** in the dashboard. You have three options:

| Option | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy Domain** | You want to register a new domain through Pxxl. |
| **Add Domain** | You already own the domain somewhere else. |
| **Transfer Domain** | You want to move the registration to Pxxl. You need the EPP code from your current registrar. |

## Connect a domain you own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Open **Domains**, choose **Add Domain**, and enter the name, for example `example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a project">
    Pick the project that should receive the traffic. You can also choose **Connect later**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Pxxl shows the records to add. Either add them at your current DNS provider, or switch your nameservers to Pxxl. See [DNS and nameservers](/domains/dns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Select **Verify Setup**. Pxxl issues the TLS certificate once the records resolve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also connect a domain from the CLI:

```bash
pxxl domains connect example.com --project <project-id>
```

<Note>
  DNS changes can take a few minutes to a few hours to spread. If verification fails right away, wait and try again.
</Note>

## Buy a domain

Choose **Buy Domain**, search for a name, and pay from your card or [wallet](/account/wallet). Pxxl registers it, hosts its DNS, and lets you connect it to a project in one step. Domains bought on Pxxl can also have managed subdomains.

## Domain page tabs

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **DNS** | Add and edit records when Pxxl hosts the zone. |
| **Nameservers** | Shows who controls the zone and how to switch to Pxxl. |
| **Routing** | Connects a project, manages subdomains, and controls the edge proxy. |
| **Monitoring** | Traffic, edge requests, and certificate status. |
| **Settings** | Connected project, proxy rules, transfer, and removal. |

## Resync the proxy

After you change the connected project, the app port, or proxy settings, select **Resync Proxy**. It republishes the edge route. It does not rebuild your app.
